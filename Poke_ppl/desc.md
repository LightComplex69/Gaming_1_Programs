# Poke_ppl
A diamond grid covers the background with 2 controllable Pokemon on the screen (Mega Mewtwo Y and Shiny Mega Lucario). Bounded by the edges of the screen, Lucario can be controlled with WASD keys and Mewtwo with the arrow keys. If you click on Lucario, he will enter an "angry" state where his color slowly fades into a tint of red.
