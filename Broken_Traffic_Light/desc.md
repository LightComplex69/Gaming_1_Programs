# Broken Traffic Light
An interactive drawing of a rectangle with 3 black circles along the middle lies in the middle of the screen. Clicking within any of the 3 circles will cause the circle to toggle between red (top), yellow (middle), or green (bottom) depending on which circle is clicked. Clicking anywhere outside of the 3 circles or on the white circle located in the bottom right of the rectangle will turn all circles back to black.
